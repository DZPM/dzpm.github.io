# The Archive is preserved, and pruned only by removal

The Archive Posts, 2006 to 2009, are kept as they were published: the same words, in the same voice, under a Banner that says so. Nothing in them is rewritten and nothing is added. What is not kept is removed: a line, a photo, a Comment or a sentence of one, a name shortened to what a stranger would know, a face or a number blurred in a photo. The alternatives were to publish everything (a twenty year old diary carries things about other people that were never theirs to have published) or to rewrite the parts the author would not write today (which would make the Archive a different, blander text and every reader's Wayback comparison a list of edits). Removal alone keeps the claim simple and checkable: nothing here says something it did not say then.

A removal has one of these grounds: data about other people that was not public in itself (addresses, phone numbers, documents, the faces of children, number plates); a line about a named person the author would not write today; a link to a domain that has since changed hands; a Comment that was only a troll, beyond what the Banner excuses; or the plain repair of format (a lost encoding, a dead shortcode, an emoticon drawn in punctuation, a spelling slip) which does not touch what was said. In doubt, the author decides; the Archive is his.

The site does not mark where something was removed. The Banner says in one sentence that some lines and photos are gone; the record of each removal, and what it was, stays with the Migration material (docs/adr/0006). A Comment by someone else follows the same rule, entire or by the sentence, with a bracketed marker where a piece of a sentence had to go ("[correo eliminado]") so that the sentence still reads. The Portfolio is not covered: it is the author's present voice and is rewritten at will.

## Consequences

- The Section of an Archive Post is decided by its date and cannot be overridden: the `Section:` front matter that new Notes carry (CONTEXT.md, Notes) exists for Posts written from 2026 on, and the build refuses it on a Post dated before 2012.
- A removal is a change like any other in the public repository, and in practice permanent, since the repository's history is public. Putting something back (a photo that turns up, a line cut by mistake) is a new change made from the material, not a reversal.
- The Archive Post count and the Comment count can go down and never up. The figures on the site are recomputed when they do.

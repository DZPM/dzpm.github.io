# davidarcos.net

David Arcos's personal site: a portfolio of talks and technical writing since 2012, and the archived personal blog that preceded it. Everything here is published content or the vocabulary for it.

## Language

### Content

**Post**:
One published entry on the site, whether migrated from WordPress or written new.
_Avoid_: article, entry, story

**Section**:
One of the three groups every Post belongs to: Portfolio or Archive, decided by its date, or Notes, written in the Post.
_Avoid_: category

**Legacy**:
A migrated Post kept away on purpose: it stays with the Migration material and never enters the repository or the site. Its Original URL is not redirected.
_Avoid_: draft, hidden, unpublished, deleted

**Portfolio**:
The Section for Posts from 2012 onward: talks, videos, podcasts, interviews and technical writing. A label, never an address.
_Avoid_: recent posts, tech posts, portfolio page

**Kind**:
What a Portfolio Post is, one word: charla, podcast, entrevista, mesa redonda or artículo. Shown with its emoji before the date, on the card and at the top of the Post: "Artículo del 18 de septiembre de 2026".
_Avoid_: type, format, category

**Notes**:
The Section for short Posts written new from 2026 on that are neither a talk nor an article: no Cover, no Summary, rows on the Blog under their own heading, never a card on the Home. Labelled Notas.
_Avoid_: blog posts, TILs, stream, quick posts

**Archive**:
The Section for Posts before 2012, the personal era of the blog, kept for the record. A label, never an address. Labelled Archivo.
_Avoid_: old posts, nostalgia, personal posts, archive page

**Cover**:
The single image that represents a Post in link previews and at the top of its page, and a Portfolio Post in lists too. Every Portfolio Post has one. An Archive Post has one only when a restored photo is named as such (a path on the site rather than a file in covers/), and it may carry a caption.
_Avoid_: featured image, thumbnail, hero, og image

**Date**:
The date a Post carries, and the one it is sorted by: the date of what it tells (the talk, the trip, the event), not the day it was written. A Post written later about an earlier event is dated to the event.
_Avoid_: publication date, timestamp, created

**Slug**:
The permanent identifier of a Post, unique across the whole site, and the last segment of its address.
_Avoid_: permalink, id, path

**Original URL**:
Either of the two addresses a Post had on the WordPress site: the dated one under `/blog/`, and the older dated one without it.
_Avoid_: old URL, legacy URL, WordPress URL, permalink

**Redirect stub**:
A page published at an address the WordPress site had, whose only purpose is to send the reader to the current address of what lived there.
_Avoid_: redirect, alias, forwarder

**Embed**:
A video, slide deck or audio player from another service shown inside a Post, identified in the Post by its URL alone.
_Avoid_: widget, iframe, oembed

**Tag**:
A label on a Post. A Post carries any number of Tags, including none.
_Avoid_: keyword, label, category

**Banner**:
The notice shown on every Archive Post saying it is kept as published, with some lines and photos removed.
_Avoid_: warning, disclaimer, notice

**Abstract**:
The official description of a talk, quoted inside its Post, usually in English even though the Post is in Spanish.
_Avoid_: description, excerpt, summary

**Summary**:
One Spanish sentence per Portfolio Post, shown on its card. Written by hand, never derived from the text. Archive Posts have none.
_Avoid_: excerpt, abstract, teaser, description

### Comments

**Comment**:
A reader's reply to a Post, written on the WordPress site between 2006 and 2013 and kept as a record. No new Comments can be written; the site has no comment system.
_Avoid_: reply, discussion, feedback

**Mention**:
A pingback or a trackback another site sent when it linked a Post, kept from the WordPress database: the source URL, its title, the date and an excerpt. Shown under the Comments, not counted as one. The blog's own pingbacks to itself are not Mentions.
_Avoid_: pingback and trackback as separate things, linkback

**Commenter**:
The author of a Comment, known by a display name and, optionally, a website.
_Avoid_: user, reader, author

**Avatar**:
The image shown beside a Commenter's Comments. The set of Avatars was produced once by the Migration and never grows.
_Avoid_: gravatar, identicon, profile picture

### Menéame

**Badge**:
The mark on a Post that reached Portada, showing its Meneo count.
_Avoid_: widget, menéame button

**Meneo**:
One vote on menéame.
_Avoid_: upvote, like

**Portada**:
Menéame's front page. A Post that reached it carries a Badge; a Post that was only submitted does not.
_Avoid_: published, front page

### Navigation

**Home**:
The page at the root of the site: who David is, in his voice, followed by every Portfolio Post as a card. Labelled Inicio.
_Avoid_: homepage, portada, landing page, about, portfolio page

**Blog**:
The page at `/blog/` listing every Post: the Portfolio first, as small cards, then the Notes and the Archive by year, one line each. Labelled Blog.
_Avoid_: index, archive, archivo, all posts

**Search**:
The page that finds Posts by text or by Tag, newest first.
_Avoid_: tag page

**Not found page**:
What a reader sees at an address that does not exist. It runs a Search for the words of that address.
_Avoid_: 404, error page

**Migration**:
The one-off move of every Post, Comment and Cover from WordPress to this site, finished in September 2026. After it, the repository is the only source of truth for the site.
_Avoid_: import, export, conversion

**Migration material**:
Everything the Migration used and decided: the raw exports, the copies of the old server, the scripts and the hand decisions about people and images. It lives in a private repository and never in this one.
_Avoid_: migration data, the private repo, the scripts
